🚨 The Situation

So Hype wanted to stand out in a crowded streetwear market. But the system behind performance lacked sharpness.

  • Messaging wasn’t sharp
  • Audience targeting lacked depth
  • No structured testing system
  • Inconsistent scaling performance

They didn’t need more effort. They needed precision.

🎯 My Role

  • Marketing Strategist
  • Brand Positioning Lead
  • Meta Ads Manager
  • Copywriting & Conversion Specialist

🧠 The Strategy

1️⃣ Brand Repositioning

We refined the brand’s foundation:

  • Defined who they are truly for
  • Clarified the emotional identity they sell
  • Established strong differentiation

From “just another streetwear brand” to a brand with attitude, clarity, and edge.

2️⃣ Conversion-Focused Copy

Ad messaging was rebuilt around:

  • Strong scroll-stopping hooks
  • Desire-driven storytelling
  • Clear differentiation angles
  • Direct response principles

Every ad had a purpose. Every word had intent.

3️⃣ Performance Execution

  • Structured campaign segmentation
  • Cold audience testing framework
  • Retargeting logic implementation
  • Creative rotation strategy
  • Data-driven optimization cycles

No boosting. No guesswork. Only system-based scaling.

🏆 The Outcome

  • Cleaner performance data
  • Improved ad efficiency
  • Stronger customer engagement
  • Better brand recall
  • Scalable acquisition model

So Hype transitioned from inconsistent performance to operating with marketing clarity and control.
