Fashion Brand – Hymnswear

Case Study: Growth Consulting, Performance Marketing, Funnel Architecture & Creative Direction Support.

Focus: Building a predictable and scalable growth system instead of random ad execution.

Structured Growth Funnel
Performance Driven Scaling

🚨 The Challenge

Hymnswear had good products. But the system behind growth was missing.

  • No structured customer journey
  • Weak ad creatives
  • Messaging not emotionally strong
  • Random ad testing

The brand had potential — but no predictable system.

🎯 My Role

  • Growth Consultant
  • Performance Marketing Lead
  • Funnel Architect
  • Creative Direction Support

🧠 What We Did

1️⃣ Rebuilt the Growth Funnel

We mapped the entire journey:

  • Cold Audience → Engagement → Retargeting
  • Conversion → Repeat Buyers

Instead of running one campaign, we built a layered acquisition model.

2️⃣ Creative Strategy Overhaul

We shifted focus from simple product showcase to:

  • Identity positioning
  • Lifestyle storytelling
  • Problem-solution hooks
  • Scroll-stopping first 3 seconds

Creative testing became structured — not emotional decisions.

3️⃣ Paid Media Execution

  • Broad audience testing
  • Interest stack testing
  • Aggressive retargeting
  • Budget scaling framework
  • Weekly performance review system

Every decision was performance-backed.

🏆 The Impact

  • Sales became more consistent
  • Better customer acquisition quality
  • Stronger brand perception
  • Improved funnel efficiency
  • Clear direction for scaling

Hymnswear moved from “trying ads” to operating with a growth blueprint.
